Camera System.

The heart of the Pro Series. A proprietary multi-camera architecture that captures your swing and ball flight with more precision than any other commercial system available.

Overhead ION3 Camera
Dedicated overhead camera captures impact from directly above — giving unmatched accuracy on face angle, club path, and contact point that side-facing cameras alone cannot deliver.
4 HyperClear High-Speed Cameras
Four HyperClear cameras positioned around the impact zone triangulate exact ball speed, launch angle, and spin vector in under a millisecond.
Infrared + Blue LED Tracking
Infrared sensors combined with high-speed blue LED strobes illuminate every frame of ball departure, eliminating the motion blur and measurement error of lesser systems.
Custom Computing Architecture
Purpose-built processing hardware interprets all camera feeds simultaneously, calculating shot data and rendering ball flight in true real time.

Ball Flight.

The moment club meets ball, the Pro Series renders your shot on screen instantly. The feedback loop is so tight your brain barely distinguishes it from outdoor play — and that's exactly the point.

In-Flight Tracking
Unlike systems that estimate flight from impact data, Full Swing uses in-flight tracking sensors that follow the ball through the air — delivering a path that is physically calculated, not approximated.
Realistic Ball Physics
Spin axis, backspin, sidespin, and aerodynamic drag all factor into the flight model. Your draw, fade, snap hook, or ballooning wedge — all behave exactly as they would outdoors.
Course Conditions Integration
Wind speed, elevation, firmness, and weather are all reflected in how the ball lands and rolls. Play Sawgrass in a crosswind — the physics respond accordingly.
